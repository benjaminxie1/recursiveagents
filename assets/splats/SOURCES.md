# Plant splat: what it is made of

`plant.splat` is a simulated Gaussian splat of the GridTwin plant, generated by `python -m scripts.build_splat`
(our code). Besides the tagged assets it has the balance of plant (MV skids, switchgear lineups, the substation with its H-frame, riser and line to the grid, the O&M building, roads, poles), with sun shadows and weathering baked in. Its surfaces take their colours from real photos, so the twin looks like the plant the cameras see.
`python -m scripts.render_splat_views` then renders it from every viewpoint, once per visual state, into `renders/`.
